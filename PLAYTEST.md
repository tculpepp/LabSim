# v1 Play-test Checklist

From REQUIREMENTS §14. Play through with the designer on **a laptop
(keyboard)** and **an iPad or Chromebook (touch)**. Tick each box, and
write down anything confusing, too hard, or not fun. Those notes become
requirement updates or "After v1" ideas.

Tester: ________  Device: ________  Date: ________  Version: ________

## 1. Make a scientist
- [ ] Change body, skin, hair, clothes, extras, and pet.
- [ ] Try a rude name with the keyboard. It must be blocked kindly.
- [ ] Build a name with the word picker, then try **Surprise me!**
- [ ] Pick Junior, then later switch to Expert in **Settings**. Fun facts and questions get harder.

## 2. Walk the lab
- [ ] Visit all 9 open rooms. The Robot Workshop door is locked.
- [ ] The map (M / **Map**) shows where you are.
- [ ] Touch: the joystick feels good and the **Use** button is easy to reach.

## 3. Grab things
- [ ] Pick up items. The backpack fills, and you see a message when it's full.
- [ ] Sell something to Tom.

## 5. Jobs
- [ ] Day 1: Gizmo's tutorial leads you to Professor Emily, and you complete the first jobs.
- [ ] Get paid for a delivery job and an experiment job.
- [ ] Next day: new jobs appear.

## 6. Shop and My Lab
- [ ] Buy one thing in each tab: Tools, Outfits, Decor, Bags, Rooms.
- [ ] A tool unlocks something new (Spark Kit adds materials, the Super Microscope adds slides, the Telescope adds planets).
- [ ] Wear a new outfit from the wardrobe.
- [ ] Place, move, and put away a decoration in My Lab.
- [ ] The Robot Workshop opens after you buy it.

## 7. Saving
- [ ] Close the tab or window mid-game, reopen it, and pick **Continue**. Everything is still there.
- [ ] **Start Over** asks first, and "No" keeps your game.

## 8. Install and offline
- [ ] Install it: iPad Safari **Share → Add to Home Screen**; Chrome **Install** icon.
- [ ] Turn on airplane mode, open it from the home screen, and play.

## Grown-up checks
- [ ] Sound volume, mute, day length, and touch-control settings work and are remembered.
- [ ] **Show speed (FPS)** stays near 60 while walking, on the slowest device.
- [ ] `make size` is under 15 MB gzipped.
